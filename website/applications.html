Real-World Use Cases

Where Self-Aligning Antennas Matter

Any scenario with a directional link and a moving or hard-to-access endpoint benefits from automatic alignment.

Wireless Communication

Point-to-point links between buildings or towers that need consistently maximized signal strength.

Defense Communication

Rapid, unattended re-alignment for tactical field links where manual pointing is not always possible.

Remote Monitoring

Reliable telemetry links to sensors and equipment in remote or hard-to-reach locations.

Industrial Communication

Stable wireless links across large industrial plants, even amid metal structures and interference.

IoT Networks

Gateway antennas that automatically track the strongest path to distributed IoT nodes.

Rural Internet Connectivity

Long-range last-mile links to rural households, self-correcting for wind, settling, or terrain shifts.

ANTENNAALIGNMENT SYSTEM

An ESP32 DevKit-based Wi-Fi RSSI alignment prototype that scans a 0°–180° range and returns to its strongest measured direction.
