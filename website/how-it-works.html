System Workflow

From Wi-Fi RSSI Scan to Best Alignment

The ESP32 connects to one Wi-Fi source, compares RSSI across 0°–180° scan positions, and commands the servo to the strongest measured angle.

01

Wi-Fi Source

The current test uses the phone hotspot named realme12+5G. An ESP32-WROOM-32E source belonged to the original dual-source concept.

02

Connect Receiver

The ESP32 DevKit connects to the selected access point; only one source is used in the current configuration.

03

Read Wi-Fi RSSI

While connected, the ESP32 reads the access point's RSSI in dBm with WiFi.RSSI().

04

Scan Positions

The MG996R positional servo moves the antenna platform from 0° to 180° in 15° increments.

05

Settle and Sample

Allow the signal to settle at each angle, then collect RSSI readings. Averaging multiple samples depends on firmware support.

06

Evaluate Best RSSI

Compare readings; the highest numerical (least-negative) dBm value is the strongest measured signal and its angle is retained.

Align and Report

The servo returns to the best measured angle and the 16×2 LCD reports scan information and that the best direction was found.
